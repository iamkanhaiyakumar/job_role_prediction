🧮 How Match Score is Calculated

Transparent 5-Factor Career Fit

We combine multiple factors so your prediction is balanced and trustworthy

⚡ Direct Skill Alignment 40% Weight
How closely your current technical skills match the core requirements of the job role.
🤖 AI Model Confidence 35% Weight
Calibrated prediction likelihood from our trained 180-tree Random Forest model.
🎓 Academic Background & CGPA 15% Weight
Relevance of your degree program, academic major, and overall performance.
💼 Work / Internship Experience 5% Weight
Years of industry exposure compared against entry or mid-level expectations.
🌐 Industry Preference & Certifications 5% Weight
Your preferred career domain and verified professional certifications.
