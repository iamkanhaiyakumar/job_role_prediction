Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about multi-sector predictions, qualification gating, and calibration

Edu2Job uses a 220-tree Random Forest classifier calibrated on 23,250 realistic multi-sector profiles across 20 industries. It evaluates token-bounded skills, academic specialization, years of experience, and certifications without bias toward any single sector.

Certain professions (Doctors, Lawyers, Architects, Chartered Accountants, University Lecturers) legally require specific academic degrees (e.g. MBBS, LLB, B.Arch, Master's/PhD). For these roles, Edu2Job checks prerequisite degree eligibility. If ineligible, the match is clamped and flagged with an advisory.

Yes! For open professions like Data Analyst, Full Stack Developer, Business Analyst, Digital Marketer, or Supply Chain Analyst, Edu2Job rewards verified practical skills and transition certifications regardless of your undergraduate major.

Yes! Simply click "⚡ Upload Resume PDF" on the Predict tab. Our parser extracts your degree, major, CGPA, years of experience, and 250+ normalized skills using strict token-boundary matching with zero false positives.

Raw machine learning probabilities are often overconfident or underconfident. We apply temperature scaling optimization (Platt scaling) with T = 0.2043 to ensure predicted probabilities match real-world empirical likelihoods.
