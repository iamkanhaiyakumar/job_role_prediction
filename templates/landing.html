About Project

Edu2Job Prediction System

Bridging Education and Employment with AI

Edu2Job is an intelligent career prediction system that leverages Machine Learning to bridge the gap between education and employment. By analyzing a student's academic background, technical skills, certifications, and industry preferences, our system predicts the most suitable job roles.

The project uses a Random Forest Classifier trained on a comprehensive dataset of 10,000+ career profiles. With multi-label encoding for skills and certifications, and label encoding for categorical features, the model achieves high accuracy in predicting across 50+ job role categories.

Built with Flask for the backend, MySQL for data persistence, and a modern glassmorphic UI — Edu2Job combines the power of data science with elegant design to deliver a premium career guidance experience.
